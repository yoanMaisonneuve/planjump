The problem

While one person searches,
everyone waits

Depending on how strong your phone is, the page goes completely white every time you zoom or scroll. It's pure hell. A curtain wall installer, September 2026 (translated from French)

At a site meeting, the question comes up: what's the detail right there? Someone pulls out a phone or a tablet and starts scrolling. The superintendent waits. The project coordinator waits. The architect, the foreman and the project manager wait. Next time, count the people around the table.

The drawing already says what you need to know: wall MR03 points to sheet A-203, section 5 points to detail 5 on A-300. Those callouts are on every sheet, and on a phone you have to follow them by hand: scrolling, pinching, getting lost, then finding your way back.

The drawing carries the information. The PDF just won't let you navigate it.

Built for the job site

Two things that matter
on a job site

  • It works with no signal. Once the page is open, the app runs offline. In a concrete basement, that's the difference between a tool and an icon.
  • It doesn't guess. An ambiguous callout opens a choice instead of sending you to the wrong place. An unreadable callout simply can't be tapped. On a job site, a wrong answer costs more than no answer.
